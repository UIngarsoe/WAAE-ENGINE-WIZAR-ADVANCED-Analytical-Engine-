# 📖 Introduction

Artificial Intelligence is becoming one of the most powerful technologies in human history.

Many people ask:

> **"Can we completely trust AI if it occasionally makes mistakes?"**

The SSISM answer is simple.

**We do not trust AI alone.**

We trust the **system built around AI.**

AI is only one component.

Human ethics.

Evidence.

Verification.

Transparency.

These are what create trustworthy intelligence.
