## 🏛️ 1. PĀLI REFERENCE & CANONICAL CORRELATION (ပါဠိတော် အကိုးအကား)

### Primary Canonical Anchor:
* **Khantī Paramaṁ Tapo Titikkhā** (*Dhammapada, Verse 184*)
  > *"Khantī paramaṁ tapo titikkhā, Nibbānaṁ paramaṁ vadanti buddhā."*  
  > (Patience/Forbearance is the highest austerity; the Buddhas declare Nibbāna to be supreme.)

### Secondary Canonical Anchor:
* **Lokadhamma Sutta** (*Aṅguttara Nikāya 8.6*)
  > *"Phuṭṭhasa lokadhammehi, cittaṁ yassa na kampati."*  
  > (When touched by the conditions of the world, whose mind remains unshaken, sorrowless, stainless, and secure.)

## 🇬🇧 3. ENGLISH TRANSLATION & EPISTEMIC ANALYSIS

> "Whosoever touches or interacts with you in any manner—whether through favor or adversity—the mind that perceives this interaction as an opportunity to fulfill one's Perfections (*Pāramī*) is indeed the Mind of Higher Knowledge (*Vijjā Citta*), O Disciples.
>
> When one thinks: 'This individual has come to test and cultivate my capacity for endurance (*Khantī*); this is the precise time for me to learn the Dhamma of forbearance; let me express gratitude to the one who has come to complete my Perfections'—from that very day onward, may you acquire the strength and the methodology to advance further in your spiritual practice.
>
> Sādhu... Sādhu... Sādhu."
