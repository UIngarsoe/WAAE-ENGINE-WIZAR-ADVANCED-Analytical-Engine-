1. Prisoner's Dilemma Matrix
1.1 Classic Prisoner's Dilemma
Two players (A and B) each choose to Cooperate or Defect. The payoff structure creates a tension between individual rationality and collective welfare.
|  | B Cooperate | B Defect |
|---|---|---|
| A Cooperate | Reward, Reward (R, R) | Sucker, Temptation (S, T) |
| A Defect | Temptation, Sucker (T, S) | Punishment, Punishment (P, P) |
 * Ordering condition: T > R > P > S (and usually 2R > T + S).
 * Mutual defection (P, P) is the unique pure-strategy Nash Equilibrium even though mutual cooperation is Pareto-superior.
1.2 Extraction-Augmented Prisoner's Dilemma (Classroom 36)
In the WAAE Classroom 36 model, an Extraction Surplus term (\Delta) is embedded exclusively under mutual defection (prolonged conflict). This transforms the strategic incentive structure.
> မြန်မာ: အမြတ်ထုတ်မှုစနစ် ပေါင်းစပ်ထည့်သွင်းထားသော အကျဉ်းသားများ၏ ဒွိဟပြဿနာ ဇယားကွက်။
> နှစ်ဖက်စလုံး တိုက်ပွဲဆက်လက်ဆင်နွှဲသည့်အခါ (Mutual Defection) အာမခံကြေး အမြတ်ထုတ်မှုတန်ဖိုး (\Delta) ကို အပိုဆုလာဘ်အဖြစ် ထည့်သွင်းထားသည်။ စစ်ပွဲဆက်တိုက်ခြင်းသည် အုပ်ချုပ်သူဘက်အတွက် ဖုံးကွယ်ထားသော ငွေကြေးအမြတ်ကို ဖန်တီးပေးသည်။
> 
 * Consequence: Mutual Defection becomes even more strongly dominant for the regime side, locking the system into prolonged warfare as a capital-accumulation mechanism rather than a pure security operation.
2. Nash Equilibrium
A strategy profile is a Nash Equilibrium if no player can increase their own payoff by unilaterally deviating, given the strategies of the others. In other words: each player is playing a best response to the others.
> မြန်မာ: နက်ရှ် ညီမျှမှု အခြေအနေ ဆိုသည်မှာ တစ်ဦးချင်းစီသည် အခြားသူများ၏ ရွေးချယ်မှုကို ပြောင်းလဲခြင်းမရှိဘဲ မိမိ၏ မဟာဗျူဟာကို ပြောင်းလဲလိုစိတ် မရှိတော့သည့် တည်ငြိမ်သော အခြေအနေဖြစ်သည်။
> 
 * In the classic Prisoner's Dilemma, the unique pure-strategy Nash Equilibrium is (\text{Defect}, \text{Defect}).
 * In the Extraction-augmented version, the addition of \Delta under mutual defection strengthens this equilibrium, making continued conflict a more robust and attractive outcome for the regime.
3. Mixed Strategy Nash Equilibrium
| Type | Definition |
|---|---|
| Pure Strategy | A player chooses one action with probability 1. |
| Mixed Strategy | A player randomizes over actions according to a probability distribution. |
A Mixed Strategy Nash Equilibrium is a profile of mixed strategies such that each player's mixture is a best response to the others. No player has an incentive to change their probability distribution.
> မြန်မာ: ရောနှော မဟာဗျူဟာ နက်ရှ် ညီမျှမှု — မဟာဗျူဟာများကို ဖြစ်နိုင်ခြေ (probability) ဖြင့် ရောနှောရွေးချယ်ထားသော တည်ငြိမ်အခြေအနေ။ တစ်ဦးချင်းစီသည် အခြားသူ၏ ဖြစ်နိုင်ခြေကို ပြောင်းလဲခြင်းမရှိဘဲ မိမိ၏ ဖြစ်နိုင်ခြေကို ပြောင်းလဲလိုစိတ် မရှိတော့ပါ။
> 
Nash’s Theorem guarantees that every finite game has at least one Nash Equilibrium in mixed strategies.

5. Zero-Sum Game Theory
A game is zero-sum if the sum of all players’ payoffs is identically zero (or a constant) for every outcome. One player’s gain is exactly another player’s loss.
> မြန်မာ: Zero-Sum Game ဆိုသည်မှာ ကစားသမားတစ်ဦး၏ အမြတ်သည် အခြားကစားသမား၏ ဆုံးရှုံးမှုနှင့် အတိအကျ တူညီသော ဂိမ်းဖြစ်သည်။ စုစုပေါင်း payoff သည် အမြဲ သုညဖြစ်သည်။
> 
Zero-Sum vs Non-Zero-Sum Comparison
| Feature | Zero-Sum | Non-Zero-Sum |
|---|---|---|
| Total Payoff | Always 0 (or constant) | Can be positive or negative |
| Interests | Strictly opposed | May partially align |
| Cooperation | Impossible / meaningless | Possible but may be unstable |
| Solution Concept | Minimax / Value of the Game | Nash Equilibrium |
| Example | Matching Pennies, Chess | Prisoner's Dilemma, Extraction models |
 * Classroom 36 note: The Extraction-augmented Prisoner's Dilemma is Non-Zero-Sum because the surplus \Delta makes total payoff positive under mutual defection. The regime captures an additional financial windfall that is not a pure transfer from the other side.

 * In non-zero-sum games (including Classroom 36 models), the Minimax Theorem does not apply directly; we rely on Nash Equilibrium instead.
