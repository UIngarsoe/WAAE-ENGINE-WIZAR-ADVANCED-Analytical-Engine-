2. Core Economic Diagnostic: The Vicious Cycle
The Myanmar economic crisis is driven by a fundamental structural misalignment between Foreign Exchange (FX) policy and political security.
The FX Stress Loop
Stage	Mechanism
1. Primary Trigger	Global oil price volatility meets an inherent trade deficit.
2. Policy Failure	Short-term import restriction creates “Second-Round Repercussion Effects.”
3. Cascading Collapse	Reduced imports → Raw material scarcity → Production decline → Export revenue depletion → Further FX shortage.
4. The Vicious Cycle	Each stage reinforces the next, locking the economy into continuous FX stress.

Asset Flight
With limited legal avenues for capital preservation, citizens engage in risk-shifting, moving funds into real assets (land, vehicles) which distorts pricing for basic utility into luxury-tier inflation.
